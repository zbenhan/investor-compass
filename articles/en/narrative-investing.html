Narrative Investing Analysis: How Stories Drive Asset Prices?

Narrative Investing 2025-12-20

What is Narrative Investing?

Narrative investing is an investment strategy that focuses on how stories, emotions, and social narratives in the market affect asset prices. Unlike traditional fundamental analysis or technical analysis, narrative investing believes that investors' decisions are often driven by the stories they believe in, not just rational analysis.

The Power of Narratives: Why Do Stories Affect Asset Prices?

There are several reasons why narratives can influence asset prices:

  • Cognitive Bias: The human brain naturally loves stories and is easily influenced by vivid narratives rather than dry data
  • Social Transmission: Stories are highly contagious and can spread quickly through social media, news media, and other channels
  • Emotional Driving: Narratives can stimulate investors' greed, fear, and other emotions, which in turn affect their buying and selling decisions
  • Herd Behavior: When enough investors believe in the same story, it creates a herd effect, pushing prices in the same direction

Review of Classic Narrative Investing Cases

1. The Internet Bubble (Late 1990s)

In the late 1990s, the narrative that "the internet will change everything" spread rapidly in the market. Investors believed that internet companies would completely revolutionize business models, and stock prices were driven to astronomical levels even though these companies were not yet profitable. Eventually, the bubble burst, and many companies' stock prices plummeted.

2. Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Craze (2017 and 2021)

Bitcoin's narrative evolved from "digital gold" to "future currency," attracting a large number of investors. In 2017 and 2021, Bitcoin prices experienced two major surges followed by sharp declines, fully demonstrating the enormous impact of narratives on asset prices.

3. Tesla's "Sustainable Energy Revolution" Narrative

Tesla, with its narrative of "accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy," saw its stock price rise dozens of times in just a few years, far exceeding the combined market value of traditional automakers. Investors were attracted by founder Elon Musk's vision, ignoring traditional valuation metrics.

Narrative Life Cycle: From Emergence to Disillusionment

Every market narrative has its life cycle, which typically includes four stages:

1. Emergence Stage

A new narrative begins to emerge, usually proposed by a few people. At this time, market attention to this narrative is low, and asset prices change little.

2. Diffusion Stage

The narrative begins to spread widely through media, social media, and other channels, and more and more investors begin to pay attention to and believe in this narrative. Asset prices start to rise significantly.

3. Mania Stage

The narrative reaches its peak, and almost all investors are talking about and believing in this story. Asset prices experience bubble-like increases, with valuations far exceeding reasonable levels.

4. Disillusionment Stage

As the gap between reality and narrative grows, investors begin to doubt the authenticity of the story. The narrative begins to collapse, and asset prices fall sharply, returning to reasonable valuations.

How to Identify Valuable Investment Narratives?

Identifying valuable investment narratives requires considering several factors:

1. Narrative Credibility

Evaluate whether the narrative has factual basis and conforms to basic economic laws and business logic. Avoid narratives that clearly violate common sense.

2. Narrative Transmission Power

Observe the speed and scope of the narrative's spread on social media, news media, and other channels. Narratives with strong transmission power are more likely to influence the market.

3. Narrative Sustainability

Evaluate whether the narrative has long-term sustainability or is just a short-term hot spot. Long-term narratives are more likely to bring sustained investment opportunities.

4. Narrative-Fundamental Match

Although narratives can temporarily push prices away from fundamentals, in the long run, prices will eventually return to value. Choosing narratives that match fundamentals is more likely to yield long-term returns.

Narrative Investing Strategies and Techniques

1. Narrative Monitoring

Establish a system to monitor major narratives in the market, including social media analysis, news monitoring, investor sentiment surveys, etc.

2. Narrative Verification

Conduct in-depth analysis of monitored narratives to verify their credibility and sustainability.

3. Timing

Enter in the emergence or early diffusion stage of the narrative, gradually exit in the mania stage, and avoid being caught in the disillusionment stage.

4. Risk Control

Set strict stop-loss mechanisms, don't get carried away by narratives, and maintain a rational investment attitude.

Analysis of Advantages and Disadvantages of Narrative Investing

Advantages

  • Can capture investment opportunities that traditional analysis methods cannot find
  • Sensitive to changes in market sentiment, can adjust strategies in a timely manner
  • Suitable for rapidly changing market environments and emerging industries

Disadvantages

  • Subjective, different investors may have different interpretations of the same narrative
  • Difficult to quantify and systematize
  • Easily misled by false narratives
