Reviewing 2020 Tesla: Intense Collision of Technical Analysis, Narrative and Value

Comprehensive Comparison 2026-01-11

I. 2020 Tesla Market Review

In 2020, Tesla's stock experienced an epic rally, rising from approximately $90 at the beginning of the year to approximately $700 at year-end, an increase of over 700%. This rally became one of the most classic cases in investment history, demonstrating different perspectives and decisions of different investment schools when facing the same asset.

1. Market Overview

Tesla's stock price trend in 2020 can be divided into several stages:

  • January-March: Stock price fluctuated between $90-100
  • April-June: Stock price began to rise, breaking through $200
  • July-August: Stock price accelerated upward, breaking through $400
  • September-October: Stock price pulled back to around $300
  • November-December: Stock price rose again, breaking through $700

2. Key Events

Several key events occurred in 2020 that drove Tesla's stock price higher:

  • January: Tesla was included in the S&P 500 index
  • April: Tesla announced three consecutive quarters of profitability
  • June: Tesla's market value exceeded Toyota's
  • August: Tesla announced a 1:5 stock split
  • December: Tesla announced inclusion in the S&P 500 index

3. Market Sentiment

Market sentiment underwent dramatic changes in 2020:

  • Beginning of year: Market was cautious about Tesla
  • Mid-year: Market sentiment turned optimistic
  • End of year: Market sentiment reached euphoria

II. Technical Analysis Perspective

1. Technical Patterns

From a technical analysis perspective, Tesla's stock price in 2020 formed typical technical patterns:

  • Breakout Pattern: Stock price broke through long-term consolidation range in April
  • Uptrend: From April onwards, stock price formed a strong uptrend
  • Pullback Confirmation: September's pullback confirmed the uptrend
  • Second Breakout: November's breakout confirmed continuation of the uptrend

2. Technical Indicators

Technical indicators provided clear trading signals in 2020:

  • Moving Averages: 50-day MA and 200-day MA formed golden cross
  • RSI: RSI indicator showed overbought, but stock price continued to rise
  • MACD: MACD indicator continued to issue buy signals
  • Volume: Volume continued to expand during the rally

3. Trading Strategy

Technical analysis traders' trading strategy in 2020:

  • Breakout Trading: Entered after breakout in April
  • Trend Following: Held during the uptrend
  • Buy on Pullback: Added positions during September pullback
  • Stop Loss Setting: Set stop loss to control risk

4. Limitations of Technical Analysis

Technical analysis also faced some limitations in 2020:

  • Overbought Signals: RSI indicator repeatedly showed overbought, but stock price continued to rise
  • Valuation Pressure: Technical analysis cannot assess valuation levels
  • Fundamental Changes: Technical analysis cannot predict fundamental changes
  • Narrative Impact: Technical analysis cannot assess narrative impact

III. Narrative Investing Perspective

1. Core Narratives

Core narratives for Tesla in 2020 included:

  • EV Revolution: Tesla leading the electric vehicle revolution
  • Autonomous Driving: Tesla's autonomous driving technology leading
  • Energy Revolution: Tesla as leader in energy revolution
  • Musk Effect: Musk's personal charm and influence

2. Narrative Evolution

Tesla's narrative evolved in 2020:

  • Beginning of year: Narrative mainly focused on electric vehicle sector
  • Mid-year: Narrative expanded to autonomous driving and energy sectors
  • End of year: Narrative further expanded to technology and AI sectors

3. Narrative Dissemination

Tesla's narrative spread through various channels:

  • Social Media: Musk's influence on Twitter
  • Media Coverage: Extensive media coverage of Tesla
  • Analyst Reports: Analysts' optimistic predictions for Tesla
  • Investor Discussions: Investor discussions on social media

4. Narrative Investing Strategy

Narrative investors' strategy in 2020:

  • Identify Narrative: Identify Tesla's core narrative
  • Evaluate Narrative Strength: Evaluate narrative's spread and influence
  • Track Narrative Evolution: Track narrative's evolution and expansion
  • Utilize Narrative: Use narrative for investment decisions

IV. Value Investing Perspective

1. Valuation Analysis

From a value investing perspective, Tesla's valuation in 2020 was extremely high:

  • Price-to-Earnings Ratio: P/E ratio exceeded 1000x
  • Price-to-Sales Ratio: P/S ratio exceeded 20x
  • Price-to-Book Ratio: P/B ratio exceeded 30x
  • Discounted Cash Flow: DCF valuation showed stock price was overvalued

2. Fundamental Analysis

Tesla's fundamentals improved in 2020:

  • Profitability: Three consecutive quarters of profitability
  • Deliveries: Annual deliveries approached 500,000 vehicles
  • Market Share: Market share in electric vehicle market continued to increase
  • Capacity Expansion: Capacity continued to expand

3. Value Investing Dilemma

Value investors faced dilemmas in 2020:

  • Overvaluation: Valuation far exceeded traditional value investing standards
  • Growth Expectations: Market had extremely high growth expectations for Tesla
  • Narrative Impact: Narrative impact on stock price far exceeded fundamentals
  • Traditional Method Failure: Traditional valuation methods seemed to fail

4. Value Investing Strategy

Value investors' strategy in 2020:

  • Avoid Investment: Avoid investing in overvalued Tesla
  • Short Strategy: Some value investors chose to short
  • Wait for Opportunity: Wait for valuation to return to reasonable levels
  • Adjust Methods: Some value investors adjusted valuation methods

V. Collision of Different Investment Schools

1. Technical Analysis vs Value Investing

Technical analysis and value investing collided on Tesla:

  • Technical Analysis: Focused on price behavior and trends
  • Value Investing: Focused on intrinsic value and valuation
  • Conflict: Technical analysis suggested buying, value investing suggested avoiding
  • Result: Technical analysis traders gained profits, value investors missed opportunity

2. Narrative Investing vs Value Investing

Narrative investing and value investing collided on Tesla:

  • Narrative Investing: Focused on narrative and story
  • Value Investing: Focused on fundamentals and valuation
  • Conflict: Narrative investing suggested buying, value investing suggested avoiding
  • Result: Narrative investors gained profits, value investors missed opportunity

3. Technical Analysis vs Narrative Investing

Technical analysis and narrative investing complemented each other on Tesla:

  • Technical Analysis: Provided entry and exit signals

  • Complement: Two methods complemented each other
